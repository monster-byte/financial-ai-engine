# Quantitative Financial Reasoning

The goal of this layer is to enhance general AI reasoning
with quantitative financial analysis.

## Initial Objectives

- Convert qualitative market signals into measurable metrics.
- Calculate financial indicators.
- Combine multiple signals.
- Produce confidence scores.
- Identify contradictions between signals.
- Explain quantitative conclusions in natural language.

## Initial Metrics

- Market breadth
- Volatility regime
- Trend strength
- Interest-rate sensitivity
- USD sensitivity
- Correlation
- Risk score
- Confidence score
