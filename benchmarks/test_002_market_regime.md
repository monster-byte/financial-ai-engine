# Test 002 — Market Regime Detection

## Objective

Determine the current financial market regime from multiple
market and macroeconomic signals.

## Scenario

- Equity trend: bullish
- Market breadth: 78%
- Volatility: moderate
- Interest rates: rising
- USD: strengthening
- Credit spreads: stable
- Technology sector: outperforming
- Commodities: mixed
- Economic growth: slowing

## Questions

1. What is the most likely market regime?
2. Which signals support the regime?
3. Which signals contradict it?
4. What are the main risks?
5. What additional data is required?
6. Assign probabilities to the three most likely regimes.
7. Explain the reasoning behind the probabilities.

## Evaluation Criteria

- Quantitative reasoning
- Multi-factor analysis
- Contradiction detection
- Macro-market integration
- Risk awareness
- Probability calibration
- Quality of explanation
