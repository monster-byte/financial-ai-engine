# Test 001 — Market Reasoning

## Task

Analyze a hypothetical NASDAQ-100 market scenario and explain
the reasoning behind the conclusion.

## Scenario

- NASDAQ-100 trend: bullish
- Volatility: moderate
- Interest rates: unchanged
- USD: stable
- Market breadth: positive
- Technology sector: outperforming

## Questions

1. What is the overall market regime?
2. What are the strongest bullish factors?
3. What are the main risks?
4. What additional information is required before making a decision?
5. Give a confidence score from 0 to 100.

## Evaluation

The model will be evaluated on:

- Financial reasoning
- Consistency
- Risk awareness
- Ability to identify missing information
- Quality of explanation
